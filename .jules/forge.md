## 2026-11-02 - Dependency and Packaging Modernization

**Observation:** The `Tedd.ReadOnlyStructGenerator.Archive` and `Tedd.ReadOnlyStructGenerator.Benchmarks` projects are generating NuGet packages during `dotnet pack`, leading to warnings about missing readmes. These are auxiliary projects and should not be packed. Test dependencies (`Microsoft.NET.Test.Sdk`, `coverlet.collector`, `xunit.runner.visualstudio`) and benchmark dependencies (`Microsoft.CodeAnalysis.CSharp`) are outdated. `xunit` 2.9.3 is deprecated in favor of `xunit.v3`, but upgrading to a functionally different package requires authorization. `Microsoft.CodeAnalysis` packages in the main generator project must remain at 4.14.0 for .NET 8 SDK support to prevent `CS9057`.

**Strategic Action:** Added `<IsPackable>false</IsPackable>` to `Tedd.ReadOnlyStructGenerator.Archive.csproj` and `Tedd.ReadOnlyStructGenerator.Benchmarks.csproj`. Updated `coverlet.collector` to 10.1.0, `Microsoft.NET.Test.Sdk` to 18.11.0, and `xunit.runner.visualstudio` to 4.0.1 in the test project. Updated `Microsoft.CodeAnalysis.CSharp` to 5.9.0 in the benchmarks project. Retained `xunit` at 2.9.3 pending authorization for v3 migration. Retained `Microsoft.CodeAnalysis.*` at 4.14.0 in the generator and archive projects.
